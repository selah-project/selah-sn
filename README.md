# Tanakh — kududzirwa muchiShona (selah-sn)

Uku ndiko kududzirwa kwemagwaro matsvene echiHebheru (Tanakh) muchiShona. Ndima
imwe neimwe iri mufaira rayo, uye izwi rimwe nerimwe rakanyorwa rakatarisana
neizwi rechiHebheru raro.

**Kunyorwa ndiko kwechiShona rakamira — mavara echiRatini chete, kubva kuruboshwe
kuenda kurudyi.** Hapana zviratidzo pamusoro pemavara (`á é í ó ú`) — izvozvo
ndezvedura remazwi, kwete ndima — uye hapana `ñ`.

> **Chinyorwa ichi chakaitwa nemuchina, uye chakamirira nzeve yechiShona
> yechokwadi.** Panzvimbo yose apo chiShona chakaoma, chisina kunaka, kana
> chisinganzwiki semataurirwo evanhu — **chinofanira kugadziriswa.** ChiHebheru
> riri pedyo neizwi rimwe nerimwe, saka unogona kuona.

Asi **verenga `CONTRIBUTING.md` usati wagadzirisa chinhu.** Zvimwe zvinoita
sezvakakanganiswa zvakaitwa nemaune, uye zvinotsanangurwa imomo.

## ChiHebheru chiri pamberi pezvose

Kududzirwa uku kuripo kuti **kukuendese kuchiHebheru** — kwete kuti kutsive
chiHebheru. Chinoverengwa nguva dzose ndiko chiHebheru: mavara aro, inzwi raro,
zviratidzo zvaro. Chinyorwa chechiShona ndiro hwindo, kwete musuwo wokupedzisira.

## Zita: `Yahwe`

`יהוה` rinonyorwa **`Yahwe`**.

Kwete `Jehovha`. Kwete `Mwari` pachinzvimbo cheZita. Kwete `Ishe`. **`Mwari` ndiro
izwi rechiShona rakanaka pamusoro paMwari — asi harisi Zita**, uye kana Zita
richitsiviwa, muverengi haakwanisi kuona kuti chiHebheru rakanyora chii.

Zvichida `Yahwe` rinoita seizwi rakachekwa kana wakaverenga chimwe chigaro
cheSelah. Harisi. **ChiShona rinovaka manzwi akavhurika** — svinu roga roga
rinopera nemubvumo — saka `Yahweh` haigoni kuva izwi rechiShona.

Ndizvo zvakare zvinoita kuti `אלהים` rive **`Erohimu`**: **chiShona rakamira
harina `l`.**

Asi **`mwari` neduku rakakodzera pamusoro pavamwari venyika** — mhuru yendarama,
Dhagoni, Baari-zebubi. Kunyora semadzitaurwa kwakachengeterwa Mumwe chete.

## `⟨את⟩`

`את` ndiwo mucherechedzo wechiHebheru unoratidza chinoitwa. **ChiShona harina
izwi rakadaro**, saka rakasiyiwa muchiHebheru, mukati me`⟨ ⟩`.

Mazwi echiShona ari mukati me`⟨ ⟩` haana kunyorwa muchiHebheru — tsika
yechiShona inoda. Zvakaonekwa kuti ukwanise kusiyanisa.

`translation` ndiwo mutsara — zvinoverengwa. `tokens` ndiwo mutsetse — izwi
rechiHebheru neizwi rechiShona pamwe chete.

**Zvose zviri zviviri zvinofanira kuenderana.** Kana mutsetse uchiti chimwe
chinhu uye mutsara uchiti chimwe, ndiko kukanganisa — uye kwakawanikwa pano:
Genesisi 5:27 mutsetse une `Metuseraki` asi mutsara une izwi risina revo.

## Zvinozivikanwa kuti hazvina kunaka

- **Eksodo 11:6, 11:7, 11:8 haana kunyorwa muchiShona** — akanyorwa muchiYidhishi.
  Ne`Eksodo 11:5`, rakabva mukukumbira kumwe chete.
- Ndima dzine mavara echiCyrillic kana echiGreek akavanda mukati memazwi.
- Ndima dzine mavara ane zviratidzo, izvo chiShona rakamira harina.
- Mafaira ane `tokens` asina chinhu — anoita seakapera asi asina mutsetse.

Zvose zvakanyorwa muna `NOTES.md` na `PROVENANCE.md`, nezvikonzero.
